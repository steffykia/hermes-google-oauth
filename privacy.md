# Privacy Policy

## Purpose

Hermes Personal Workspace is a private, single-user integration used by the owner to connect a locally operated Hermes Agent installation to the owner's Google account.

## Google data accessed

Depending on the owner's request, the application may access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files and metadata, Google Docs and Google Sheets content, and Google Contacts. The application may create or modify Google content when explicitly instructed by the owner.

## Use of information

Google account data is used only to perform actions requested by the account owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

## Storage and sharing

Authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's private system. Google account data is not sold or shared with third parties. Data may be processed by the locally operated Hermes Agent and its configured AI service providers when necessary to complete an owner-requested task.

## Data control

The owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account security settings.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
