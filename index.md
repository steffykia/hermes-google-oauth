# Hermes Personal Workspace

Hermes Personal Workspace is a private, single-user integration operated by the owner of this site.

The application connects the owner's locally operated Hermes Agent installation to the owner's Google account. It may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts when explicitly requested by the owner.

The application is not offered to the public and does not provide access to any other user's Google account.
